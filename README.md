## Research Manuscripts

### LiDAR Perception and Mapping

**When Point Counts Mislead**

Investigation of LiDAR point-cloud quality and the limitations of raw point counts as indicators of usable spatial information.

*Status: Submitted — Under Review*

### Semantics-Constrained HTTP Recovery

Investigation of fault-aware recovery strategies combining Bayesian belief estimation, operation semantics, application deadlines, and bounded POMDP planning.

Evaluated across 5,040 live HTTP episodes using a Python recovery controller and Ballerina transactional services.

*Status: Submitted — Under Review*

[Research Repository](https://github.com/Thilac01/Adaptive_HTTP_resilience_in_Ballerina)

### Higher-Dimensional Radial Model (HDRM)

Independent theoretical investigation of geometric interpretations of quantum mechanical probability distributions.

*Submitted to the International Journal of Theoretical Physics.*
